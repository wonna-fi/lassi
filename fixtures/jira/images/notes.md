direction: both
confirm: "the write direction's comma-separated `|width=…,height=…` form renders in Jira"
---
Bare images, `|thumbnail` and `key=value` parameters map; the parameters travel in the image title as
space-separated words  because pasted screenshots carry `width`/`height` in every modern Jira.
A value with spaces or quotes cannot be a title word and fences the paragraph.
